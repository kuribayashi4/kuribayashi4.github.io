Tatsuki Kuribayashi (栗林樹生)

My Japanese name, 栗林樹生, contains several kanji related to trees (木). My parents run a flower shop and love gardening, and, true to my name, I have also become fascinated by trees—linguistic ones!

My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and cognitive science. I study incremental sentence processing, discourse processing, and the reasoning processes underlying language use in humans and machines.
  • I joined MBZUAI as a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Natural Language Processing in the Division of Computing and Mathematical Sciences in August 2025.
  • I lead the Processing and Acquisition of Language in Machines and Mind (PALM) 🌴 group at MBZUAI.
